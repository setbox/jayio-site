Field note no. 1 · The method

A list for one day only

jayio is not where you keep everything you have to do. It is where what you are going to do today fits. That difference is the whole method, and it has three rules.

  1. End the day by writing tomorrow's list

    The last thing of the working day is not more work: it is looking at the calendar, seeing what tomorrow's commitments ask of you and writing the list. You arrive the next morning knowing what to do, instead of deciding it while your head is still cold.

  2. Every item has to fit in one day

    If it does not fit, it is not an item, it is a project, and a project does not go on today's list, it goes in slices. An item also has to be specific enough to leave no doubt about whether it was done.

  3. The list may grow during the day

    Did something useful that was not planned? Write it down and mark it done. The list is there to show the day as it happened, not to protect the plan you drew in the morning.

A vague item is an agreement you can break without noticing.

The first rule is a ritual: close the notebook only after turning the page. That is exactly what jayio does for you, on its own, at your midnight, and it is why there is a status called for tomorrow, and why Today wakes up empty.

        Field note no. 4 · What is not for today

        The backlog

        The backlog is where the acorn waits until the day to dig it up.

        Bury it with a date

        A task with a future date does not sit blinking in Today. It waits in the backlog and moves up on its own on the morning of the chosen day, already without the date: that way it is not promoted again at the next midnight.

        Drag, do not number

        The order is yours and it is manual: drag the item to where it belongs. Moving one card does not reorder the whole list, so a long list stays as fast as a short one, and the position you chose is the one waiting for you next time.

        The flock

        MCP

        Eight tools: create, list, read, update, move, complete, list labels and describe the schema. Your agent comes in with the same personal token you revoke on the tokens screen.

        REST

        /api/v1 with a bearer token. Scope per action: a read token stays a read token. Signing out of a device deletes only that token.

        Screen

        The turnover arrives on its own: if the tab is open when the day turns, the list rearranges in front of you. Nobody has to hit F5 to find out that tomorrow started.
